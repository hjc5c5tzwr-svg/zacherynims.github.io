The Creator

A Life Built on
Storytelling

Zachery Nims is a multi-disciplinary creative based in Katy, Texas — a storyteller who moves fluidly between the written word, the screen, the canvas, and the recording studio.

From heartwarming Christmas films to a literary novel, from children's books that spark imagination to an album born from a desert upbringing, Zach's work is united by one conviction: stories change people.

His documentary about Aramco Brats — produced with his brother and screened by royal invitation in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia — stands as a testament to the reach of authentic storytelling.

A deeply personal documentary co-created with his brother, exploring the unique experience of growing up as Aramco Brats — children raised in the oil camps of Saudi Arabia. The film was screened by royal invitation in Riyadh, a rare honour for an independent production.

Books & Scripts

Literary Novel & Screenplay

VEG

Zachery's debut novel — a work rich enough to demand its own screenplay adaptation. An ambitious creative universe told first in prose, then reimagined for the screen.

Children's Book · The Heroes Series, Book 1

The Heroes in My Closet

A story about bravery and the discovery that the greatest heroes in a child's life are right at home — their parents. Part one of the two-book Heroes Series.

Children's Book · The Heroes Series, Book 2

The Heroes Inside

The companion volume to the series, turning the lens inward. A celebration of self-confidence, reminding young readers that the most important hero lives inside them.

Children's Book · Advocacy

They Say I Have a Problem

A compassionate, thought-provoking book for children with ADD and ADHD and their families. It challenges the rush to medicate, proposing instead that these traits may be an evolutionary advantage — not a condition to be corrected.

Children's Book · Bedtime Adventure

The Sleepy Book

A magical bedtime adventure that makes reading itself part of the magic. The secret? If a child reads it just right, every book holds enough magic to put their parents to sleep. A love letter to reading.
